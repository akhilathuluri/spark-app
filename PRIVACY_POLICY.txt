Last Updated: April 2026

IF YOU DO NOT AGREE, DO NOT INSTALL OR USE THE SOFTWARE.

This Privacy Policy describes how Spark ("Software") handles data.

1. PRIVACY PRINCIPLES
1.1 We apply strict data-minimization and purpose-limitation principles.
1.2 We do not claim ownership of your content.
1.3 We do not sell personal data.

2. DATA PROCESSED BY THE SOFTWARE
2.1 User-Provided Content:
- Selected text and prompts you choose to process.
- This content may be sent to configured AI providers (for example OpenRouter
  or Groq) to generate results.

2.2 Local Application Data:
- Local settings (provider/model preferences, UI settings).
- Local history entries and usage metrics stored on your device.
- Local debug and diagnostic files when enabled.

2.3 Optional Telemetry:
- Anonymous product analytics events may be sent when telemetry is enabled.
- Telemetry does not intentionally include selected text content.

3. DATA STORAGE LOCATIONS
3.1 Local data is stored on your device (for example under AppData\Spark).
3.2 API keys are protected using Windows DPAPI where supported.

4. THIRD-PARTY PROCESSORS
4.1 When you configure an AI provider, your prompts/selected text are sent to
that provider to fulfill your request.
4.2 Third-party providers process data under their own policies and terms.
4.3 You are responsible for reviewing and complying with those third-party
policies before use.

5. STRICT USE REQUIREMENTS
5.1 Do not submit data you are not authorized to process.
5.2 Do not process regulated or restricted data unless your compliance team has
approved your usage pattern and provider configuration.

6. DATA RETENTION
6.1 Local data is retained on your device until removed by you or by uninstall.
6.2 You can remove app data by deleting local files and uninstalling the app.

7. SECURITY
7.1 We apply reasonable technical safeguards in the Software design.
7.2 No software can guarantee absolute security; you are responsible for secure
endpoint and account practices.

8. CHILDREN
Spark is not intended for use by children under the age required by applicable
law in your jurisdiction.

updates and installer packages.
